About Us

In 2021, two unassuming Utahns took a trip to the Oregon coast and found paradise. Over the next 9 months, they built a house and moved to the quiet neighborhood of North Albany in the fertile Willamette valley.

Desirous to share the beauty of this state and stay connected to their loved ones left behind, they constructed a home with a mostly-private above-the-garage junior suite to house visitors from near and far. After decking out the room with all of the amenities you'd expect from a quality hotel, they renamed this home-away-from-home The Burgener Chalet.

Whether you're a current guest, considering a luxurious stay, or just interested in connecting with the proprietors, there's something at The Burgener Chalet for you. This site is dedicated to one of the more popular amenities, a broad selection of table-top board games for visitors to play amongst themselves or with the owners.

Together, we at The Burgener Chalet extend a warm welcome, and invite you to peruse our selection, mark your favorites, check out a game or two, or schedule a game night with us.
